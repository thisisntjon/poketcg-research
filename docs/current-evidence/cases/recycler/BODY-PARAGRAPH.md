Recovery posed a different strategic choice: spend a slot on finding Pokémon, or on returning discarded Energy to the deck. For the experimental Lucario player, we replaced one Poké Pad with one Energy Recycler; both 60-card lists retained the same targeting-policy setting. Recycler offered two possible benefits: more cards before deckout and renewed access to attack Energy. Sacred Ash offered Pokémon recovery instead. Removing search was an opportunity cost in every matchup. [R01, R04, R05]

Against the targeted opponent implementation, the first 1,000-game-per-arm batch rose from 335 to 408 wins: **+7.30 percentage points, 95% interval [3.08, 11.52]**. Deckout losses fell from 664 to 592, but these aggregate changes cannot show which avoided deckouts caused wins or separate the two recovery mechanisms. [R02]

Four undecided games per arm were excluded. That panel includes the later target batch, so the summaries overlap. It established neither overall improvement nor distributed harm.
