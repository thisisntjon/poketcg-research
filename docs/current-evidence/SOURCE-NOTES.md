## [4] Representation probes

[Representation evidence and portable recount](factory/proofs/representation/README.md) supplies two retained six-row summaries, cache metadata, source identities and a standard-library verifier. The first cache contains 44,343 examples; paired validation counts are 4,308 / 4,466 / 4,579. Mean raw validation agreement is 48.616737% baseline and 74.251360% identity, rounded to 48.62% and 74.25% in the article.

The second recorded public-teacher cache contains 66,030 examples; paired validation counts are 6,862 / 6,663 / 6,742. Mean agreement is 47.332096% baseline and 77.258906% identity, rounded to 47.33% and 77.26%. The producer records 66,074 generated rows, reconciling to 66,030 cached plus 44 skipped. The [clean producer excerpt](factory/proofs/representation/second/CORPUS-PROVENANCE-EXCERPT.json) credits Roman Rozen's public V13 teacher through recorded identifiers; the loader has not been re-executed. An unrelated historical teacher-strength assertion is excluded.

Each result is an unweighted mean of three recorded seed comparisons. Inspected trainer code uses seed-dependent whole-game splits; the retained summaries do not supply assignments for independent split verification. Different recorded corpus constructions are not proof of disjoint data or independent replication. No raw overlap audit was performed.

Both identity conditions add a 16-dimensional embedding, enlarge the option-layer input from four to twenty and increase capacity by 69,632 parameters. There is no capacity-matched control. The summaries do not fully bind original trainer/runtime/checkpoint identities. The recount verifies saved metric arithmetic without rerunning training, certifying teacher correctness or attributing playing strength. The current v2 architecture is not claimed to descend directly from these July probes. Figure 1 compresses the investigations into lessons; it does not assert that one exact probe caused the separate v2 rebuild.

They check legal masking, selection bounds, no replacement, STOP, context-dependent scores and an expected refusal. No trained model or game engine is used. These cases demonstrate component behavior, not universal correctness or tactical quality. Recurrent and imitation-learning methods are established methods used by the project.

This does not isolate the labels' effect or evaluate the current v2 architecture and runtime. ## [6] Deck-policy counter and conditional value

[Methods](numerical/METHODS.md), [input provenance](numerical/SOURCE-MANIFEST.json) and the [verifier](numerical/verify_results.py) reproduce the current game-result arithmetic. The 28,000 development rows include an 8,000-game factorial and the separate 4,000-row second-machine reproduction. Targeting pools two comparisons; card and combined each pool three, using within-batch controls, decided games and inverse-variance pooling on the stated 40%-Alakazam panel. Gains are +3.62, +5.02 and +7.82 [6.08, 9.55] percentage points. All three combined comparisons are positive. Shared factorial arms make the pooled estimates dependent; the interaction does not establish synergy. Direct rounding gives 9.55, correcting the historical double-rounded 9.56 endpoint.

The distinct 8,400-row panel has seven fixed implementations, 600 games per arm/opponent, 300 per seat and win/draw/loss scores 1/0.5/0. Figure 2 gives absolute scores and changes: +20.42 and +18.75 for two Alakazam implementations; the other five average −0.47 [−2.64, +1.71]. Normal approximate intervals use unbiased sample variance/n and condition on fixed implementations and independent games, arms and cells. Analyses are descriptive without multiplicity adjustment. One Alakazam deck overlaps development under another pilot; the panel's Grimmsnarl pilot is not submitted c61.

The [historical sensitivity supplement](review-supplement/README.md) retains the separate random-effects analysis excluding the development-overlapping deck. Under draws scored as half, its historical calculation gives +2.72 [−3.29, +8.74] percentage points; the interval includes zero. That analysis uses its historical p(1−p)/n variance approximation, distinct from the current chart's sample-score variance. Its pre-run declaration chronology is qualified in the supplement; the article makes no claim that the finalized analysis was registered before any games began.

[Opponent identities](numerical/opponents.json) and the [additional retained-file inspection](numerical/retained-opponent-identity.json) identify available aliases and hashes without reconstructing every historical execution. The separate 16,000-game exact-c61 comparison has control W/L/D 3,463/4,508/29 and treatment 3,457/4,526/17. Decided-game change is −0.14 [−1.68, +1.40]; this establishes neither equivalence nor absence of cost. Terminal outcomes do not identify which individual move sequences caused each win.

The experimental Lucario lineage is makthanithin's Apache-2.0 community_1084 policy; the author's intervention is the card substitution, targeting modification and experimental comparison. [Mechanics inspection](source-evidence/receipts/source-verification.json) records source identities. Aura Jab and Mega Brave costs, damage, acceleration and reuse constraint were additionally inspected against retained card data, whose SHA-256 is a0ea63cf7adcb65d35436ce0eb390de6e2e35654a7c67c065a45f4abaa00f373. The Carmine-to-Xerosic replacement is the evaluated treatment, not another historical hardcoded list. Preferring Abra/Kadabra may require bringing it Active; these Lucario attacks do not damage a Benched target themselves. Organizer data is excluded.
