# Research methods and materials

## Recount the retained experiments

The [current numerical bundle](current-evidence/numerical/METHODS.md) supplies 28,000 applicable development records, 8,400 seven-opponent records, 16,000 exact-c61 follow-up records and 1,203 historical student attempts. These exports contain outcomes and source locators. They are transformations of existing records, not new games.

Run `python docs/current-evidence/numerical/verify_results.py`. The [contribution map](current-evidence/CONTRIBUTIONS.md) explains each question, intervention, result and limit. Historical development files include additional cohorts; do not count those as extra replications of the headline contrasts or add original and exported copies together.

## Test the deck and decision policy together

Change the card and targeting rule separately, compare each with its own batch control, and inspect the combination across opponent implementations. Development estimated +7.82 points [6.08, 9.55]; the later panel found +20.42 and +18.75 against two tested Alakazam implementations, and −0.47 [−2.64, +1.71] across five others. One development deck reappeared with another pilot. Broader benefit remains unresolved; this does not prove that general improvement is impossible.

## Read failed attempts with their conditions

[DEAD-ENDS.md](../workflow/DEAD-ENDS.md) records attempted approaches with scope limits. A weak search implementation does not establish that search as a class cannot work. [The instrument-failure account](INSTRUMENTS-THAT-LIED.md) explains how evaluations can answer the wrong question. These are historical records; inspect their sources and corrections before transferring a numerical assumption to a new setting.

## Recover a finding with its source

The [research inventory](../workflow/inventories/sri/INDEX.md) is a frozen snapshot of a larger working repository. Many linked source documents and original Git objects are outside this export. The [search example](REPRODUCING.md) works on the smaller published corpus. Neither inventory size nor a successful search is a measurement of research productivity or game strength.

Internal PR numbers and work-item names often point to private history. Current headline claims instead have direct routes through the [evidence map](EVIDENCE-MAP.md). Research-session memory, recurrent game state and trained weights are separate mechanisms.

## Scope

Organizer engine/card data, complete model weights, original teacher labels and third-party policy implementations are not included. Selected source excerpts document the learner design. Arithmetic is independently countable; complete historical execution is not reconstructed here. Attribution gaps remain disclosed in [NOTICE](../NOTICE). Generic methods can be explained and studied, while competition rules still govern Pokémon Elements and trained-model use.
